A zero-hallucination financial compiler for Indian MSMEs — every number below is produced by a rule or an equation, never a guess.

Uploads an invoice photo's OCR tokens, a GSTR-2B dump and a bank CSV, then runs the Step Functions pipeline end-to-end.

1 · Invoice compiled from pixels

OCR tokens are compiled into a typed AST. Statutory invariants either hold, are uniquely repairable (our misread), or condemn the document (supplier's error). The two are never mixed.

Synthetic demo bill — the digit in line 2's rate is genuinely blurred in the image; OCR read it as “8” at 62% confidence.

2 · One NEFT, three documents

The reconciler searches signed subsets (invoices +, credit notes −) of same-counterparty documents inside a ±90-day window, with deterministic tie-breaking.

3 · GST IMS triage & payment guardrail

A first-match-wins decision table turns the evidence into statutory IMS actions. Faulty bills get their GST held back before the money leaves.

Wrongly availed & utilised ITC attracts interest at 18% (as notified; statutory ceiling 24%) — catching this bill before filing is the whole point.

4 · Proof-of-cashflow dossier

Every record above is hashed into a SHA-256 Merkle tree (canonical JSON, leaves sorted by key) and sealed in an Object-Locked S3 bucket. Same books, same root — forever.
